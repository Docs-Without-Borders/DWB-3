Why We Exist

Notaries are essential, but rarely celebrated.

Every day, notaries stop fraud, protect elders, close homes, and give immigrants a valid voice. Yet the profession has no global stage.

Documents Without Borders Global was founded by Oswald Joseph Bien-Aimé, CEO of The Notary Giant, to build an academy, awards, and Hall of Fame that puts notaries on the world stage.
